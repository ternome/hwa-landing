About this game

Raise an alliance of 75+ legendary champions, plunge into massive PvP raids and crack the gates of the Astaroth. Customize your team, master your hero's signature combos, and fight for control of the realm in 10v10 alliance combat. Climb the ladder and you may earn the chance to be remembered as a legend.

How to Play Hero Wars

Step 1

Create your account

Sign up for free in 30 seconds. Cross-platform progress on web, iOS, Android and Steam — your alliance follows you anywhere.

Step 2

Recruit your first heroes

Open three starter summons and pick a class to lead. You'll get Galahad and a free 5★ shard pack to kick off your roster.

Step 3

Join an alliance

Tap "Find Alliance" and we'll match you with an active guild near your skill level. Now you're ready for the realm.
