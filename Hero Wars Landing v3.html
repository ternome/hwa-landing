What you'll fight for

feature.

01.

Siege the gates of the Astaroth

Coordinated alliance combat at scale. Bring 10 heroes, breach a fortress, and split the relic before the rival alliance closes the breach. Every gate is a one-shot opportunity — fail and the realm shifts.

02.

A new genre of alliance combat

The tension of competitive PvP woven into a 75+ hero roster RPG. Stalk the open realm in stealth, ambush at the crossroads, or commit to the long siege — the strategy is yours, the consequences are permanent.

03.

Realistic graphical fidelity

Built in Unreal Engine 5 — every banner, every dent, every spark from a clashing blade is hand-tuned. A grim fantasy world rendered with the production value of a feature film.

04.

Express who you are

75+ heroes, hundreds of skill paths, alliance crests, banner colors, voice lines. How far will you go to make your mark? Hero Wars is a stage — yours to claim.

Overview

Find hope in the storm of survival

Begin your journey in a sundered land where endless exploration meets endless challenge. Between the looming Astaroth and the rival alliances cutting paths across the realm, every choice is strategic — and every choice is yours.

Engage the war head-on or slip through unseen, paying the price for whichever path you pick. Loot a fallen siege, fortify your stronghold, raise a banner. Inside the brutal survival contest, write your own legend.
